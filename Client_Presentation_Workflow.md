# School Management System - Complete Business Workflow

This document outlines the end-to-end workflow of the School Management System, structured specifically for a business client presentation. It demonstrates how different stakeholders interact with the platform from initial setup to daily operations.

---

## 1. Onboarding & Multi-Tenant Setup (Super Admin)
*The journey begins with the platform owner managing multiple school clients.*

*   **Tenant Acquisition:** A new school self-registers (enters *Pending Approval* state) or is directly added by the Super Admin.
*   **Subscription & Licensing:** Super Admin assigns an appropriate subscription plan, sets feature limits, and monitors overall license usage.
*   **Branding & Configuration:** Super Admin applies custom white-labeling (logos, colors) and configures environment variables so the school feels it's using a proprietary system.

## 2. School Initialization (School Admin)
*The school's administrative team takes over to set up their specific environment.*

*   **Dashboard Access:** The School Admin logs in to their customized, branded portal.
*   **Academic Structure:** Admin creates the foundational structure: academic years, classes, sections, and subjects.
*   **Staff Onboarding:** Admin generates secure invite links or directly registers teachers and staff, managing their HR profiles and payroll details.
*   **Student Enrollment:** Admin adds student records, health data, and assigns students to their respective classes and sections.

## 3. Academic Planning & Operations (Admin & Teachers)
*Setting the stage for the academic term.*

*   **Timetable & Scheduling:** Admin creates the master timetable, intelligently assigning teachers to subjects, classes, and specific time slots without conflicts.
*   **Lesson Planning:** Teachers log in to their dedicated hub, view their assigned classes, structure daily lesson plans, and upload necessary study materials.
*   **Centralized Communication:** Admin utilizes the Noticeboard to broadcast announcements and policies to staff, students, or parents.

## 4. Daily Classroom Workflow (Teachers & Parents)
*The day-to-day engagement between educators and the student's family.*

*   **Attendance Tracking:** Teachers quickly mark daily attendance via the Teacher Dashboard; Parents receive instant updates if their child is absent.
*   **Homework Management:** Teachers assign digital homework and projects. Parents can track assigned, pending, and completed homework via the Parent Portal.
*   **Direct Engagement:** Built-in chat allows for seamless communication between teachers and parents. Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs) can be scheduled directly through the platform.

## 5. Examination & Performance Tracking (Admin, Teachers, Parents)
*Evaluating and reporting student progress.*

*   **Exam Configuration:** Admin schedules examinations and sets up custom grading rubrics.
*   **Grading & Assessment:** Teachers input marks and qualitative assessments into the system for their respective subjects.
*   **Dynamic Report Generation:** Admin uses the Report Template Builder to generate customized report cards, which are instantly published to the Parent and Student portals for review.

## 6. Financial & Logistics Management (Admin & Parents)
*Handling the operational and financial backbone of the school.*

*   **Fee Management:** Admin configures fee structures and generates invoices. Parents can view transparent fee breakdowns and track payments directly in their portal.
*   **Transport & Logistics:** Admin manages vehicle fleets, routes (with GPS tracking), and allocates hostel rooms if applicable.
*   **Inventory & Library:** Admin oversees all school assets, tracks inventory supplies, and manages the library's book issuance system.

## 7. Continuous Support & Analytics (All Stakeholders)
*Ensuring smooth operations and data-driven growth.*

*   **Issue Resolution:** Parents or students can raise issues via the Complaint Redressal module for the school to address.
*   **Platform Support:** School Admins can raise Support Tickets directly to the Super Admin for any technical assistance.
*   **Insights & Reporting:** Both Super Admins (global view) and School Admins (school-specific view) access real-time analytics and comprehensive reports to make informed, data-driven decisions.
